Flavors of Peru: Live Cooking, Peruvian Paso Horse and Nature

Much more than a tour, this is an immersion into authentic Peruvian culture, where gastronomy, nature and tradition come together in an unforgettable experience.

Just minutes from Lima, you'll discover the beautiful Lurín river valley, a natural haven where you'll enjoy an exclusive live Peruvian cooking demonstration, with traditional recipes prepared in front of you by local chefs. You'll learn about the ingredients, techniques and flavors that have made Peruvian cuisine one of the most celebrated in the world.

The experience continues with a visit to an interactive farm, walks along trails by the Lurín river, and the chance to connect with the calm of the Peruvian countryside. As a finishing touch, you'll enjoy an elegant Peruvian Paso Horse show and the passion of the traditional Marinera dance, two expressions declared Cultural Heritage of Peru.

This experience was designed for travelers who want to go beyond the traditional tourist spots and experience Peru through its flavors, its nature and its traditions, in an authentic, exclusive and unforgettable setting.

Itinerary

1
08:45 AM

Pickup from hotel / Airbnb

From Miraflores, San Isidro or Barranco. Comfortable transfer to the Cieneguilla valley (approx. 1 hour).

2
10:45 AM

Countryside Welcome

Discover a self-sufficient orchard homestead, surrounded by nature. Welcome drink of refreshing natural fruit juice, the perfect start to connect with Peruvian culture, gastronomy and nature.

3
11:00 AM

Interactive Farm 🐐

Feed and interact with sheep, hens, rabbits, guinea pigs, ducks, geese and horses. Visit a living beehive and taste fresh honey straight from the comb, and stroll through the orchard among passion fruit, papaya, cherimoya, fig and coffee plants and trees.

4
11:40 AM

Walk in the Lurín Valley 🌿

Guided walk along the natural trails of the Lurín River Valley, surrounded by vegetation, mountains and rural landscapes — a peaceful setting far removed from the pace of the city.

5
12:15 PM

Live Cooking Show 🍽️🔥

Enjoy an authentic three-course culinary experience, where the traditional flavors of Peru come to life before your eyes.

You'll then enjoy a live cooking demonstration led by a local chef, who will prepare one of Peruvian cuisine's most representative recipes step by step. As you discover the ingredients, techniques and secrets of traditional cooking, you'll be able to watch the entire preparation process, interact with the chef, ask questions and learn the story behind each dish.

At the end of the demonstration, you'll taste the freshly prepared dish, rounding out an authentic, interactive culinary experience.

6
01:10 PM

Peruvian Paso Horse & Marinera Cultural Show 💃🐎

Exhibition of the Peruvian Paso Horse alongside an experienced chalán (horseman), followed by a Peruvian Marinera performance with horse — music, tradition and elegance, both declared Cultural Heritage of Peru.

7
01:30 PM

Photo Session with the Peruvian Paso Horse 📸🐎

Capture an unforgettable memory alongside the Peruvian Paso Horse, the chalán and the marinera dancer, dressed in their traditional outfits.

8
01:40 PM

Free Time for Optional Shopping 🛍️🍯

Browse the local products shop: 100% natural honey, handmade goods and Peruvian souvenirs such as chalán hats and marinera handkerchiefs. Shopping is entirely optional.

9
01:55 PM

Return to Lima

Transfer back to your hotel/Airbnb.
